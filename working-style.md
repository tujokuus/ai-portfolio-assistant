# Working Style and Motivation — Tuomas Kuusisto

## Motivation and continuous learning

I enjoy learning new things and applying what I learn to practical problems.
I am particularly interested in artificial intelligence, machine learning,
and data engineering.

I read research papers and technical articles out of curiosity and to keep
up with developments in the field. My personal projects allow me to explore
ideas, compare approaches, and develop my technical skills through practice.

I have also attended AI meetups in Helsinki to learn from other practitioners
and hear how AI technologies are being applied.

## Responsibility and initiative at work

In my current role, I have been trusted with substantial responsibility
in a demanding professional environment. I value this trust and the
opportunity to contribute to meaningful work while developing my expertise.

I have independently moved tasks forward by exploring potential solutions
and testing promising approaches. For example, I experimented with image
analysis and then extended the approach to incorporate multimodal data,
exploring how combining different types of information could support
the task.

This reflects my approach to taking initiative: identify a possible next
step, test it in practice, and use the findings to guide further development.

## Teamwork and collaboration

My background in competitive team sports has taught me to work toward
shared goals, communicate with teammates, and take responsibility for my
contribution. It has also given me experience receiving feedback and
adapting my approach to support the team.

I have further developed my collaboration skills through volunteering
in my university's subject association, where I served as the sports
coordinator and participated in the events team. These roles gave me
experience coordinating activities with others, sharing responsibilities,
and organising events for fellow students.

These experiences have shaped how I approach teamwork: contribute
actively, listen to others, and follow through on shared commitments.

## Working under pressure

Competitive team sports have given me experience staying focused under
pressure and responding constructively when situations change. I aim to
bring the same approach to my work by focusing on the next useful action,
communicating clearly, and supporting the people around me.

## Independent project development

Alongside my professional experience, I develop personal projects to
deepen my understanding and turn ideas into working applications.

For example, I took my AI Portfolio Assistant from local development to
deployment, working on source handling, model integration, evaluation,
and the user interface. I compared different approaches to providing
source material to the model and considered their trade-offs in answer
quality, response time, cost, and implementation complexity.

I value understanding the reasons behind technical choices and choosing
solutions that fit the problem.

## Career interests

I am looking for opportunities to contribute to practical data and AI
solutions while continuing to develop my skills. I value collaboration,
constructive feedback, and learning from experienced colleagues.

I want to bring curiosity, commitment, and a willingness to take
responsibility to the team I join.
